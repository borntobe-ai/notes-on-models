# The Future of Machine Learning

English | [简体中文](./index_zh-CN.md)

Machine learning will turn digits into brains. But those brains will still need hands and legs.

LLMs generate outputs probabilistically and can hallucinate. Like other machine learning models, they can make mistakes. We should build systems that account for this fallibility rather than assume it will disappear.

What we can do is engineer dependable hands and legs: tools with clear contracts, validated inputs, and predictable behavior. No system can guarantee perfect accuracy, but well-designed tools can reject invalid actions and limit the consequences when a model chooses the wrong tool.

That is why I believe engineering will become more important, not less. Robust systems should not depend on a model's judgment alone; reliability must be built into the systems around it.

Our goal is to give AI a high probability of choosing actions that are 100% correct.
